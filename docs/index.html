The same calculation engines run on Windows and Android, so a result on the phone matches the one on the PC. Light and dark themes.

What it calculates

Seven disciplines, 38 workflows and 85 calculations. Every result shows its formula and step-by-step working, and can be saved to a project or exported as a PDF calculation sheet.

Electrical

Cable sizing and voltage drop (IEC 60364-5-52, BS 7671), load schedules, solar PV (off-grid and grid-tied), battery banks, transformers, power factor correction, short-circuit current, earthing (BS 7430, BS 7671), standby generators.

Electronics

Voltage dividers and resistor networks, RC/RL/LC filters, op-amp circuits, power supplies, PCB trace width (IPC-2221), battery runtime.

Civil

Concrete volumes and materials, beam deflection, column load (BS 8110, BS 5950), pipe flow and drainage, soil bearing and settlement, bar bending schedules, masonry and plaster.

Mechanical

Pump sizing and operating point, motor selection, shaft power and diameter, heat load and ventilation, belts, pulleys and chains.

Water & Sanitation

Water demand, storage and rainwater tanks, solar borehole pumping, septic tanks (BS 6297) and soak pits.

AI & ML

LLM memory and speed, model evaluation metrics, Edge AI hardware estimates, solar power for AI devices.

Nuclear (estimation only)

Dose rate, shielding thickness and isotope decay, with mandatory warnings. Not certified software.

Tools

Scientific calculator on every screen, projects with whole-project PDF reports, and length measurement from a photo.

How to install

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open FieldCalc.

Windows

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it to a folder, for example C:\FieldCalc.
  2. Run FieldCalc.exe.
  3. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info → Run anyway. It appears because this download isn't code-signed.

FieldCalc is in the Microsoft Store for Windows, where it updates automatically, and is coming to Google Play.

Privacy and responsible use

FieldCalc collects no data. Your calculations and projects stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

FieldCalc is a calculation aid. Have results checked by a qualified engineer before using them in design, construction or safety decisions. The nuclear module provides estimates for awareness only and is not certified software.
