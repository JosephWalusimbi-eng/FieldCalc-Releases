# FieldCalc Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 October 2026
**Publisher:** Arapai Technologies International
**Applies to:** FieldCalc for Windows and FieldCalc for Android

## Summary

FieldCalc works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no network connection.

## What the app stores, and where

| Data | Where it stays | Purpose |
|------|----------------|---------|
| Calculation inputs and results you choose to save | On your device only (local database) | Calculation history |
| PDF calculation sheets you export, including any engineer or project name you type | In the folder you export to | Your records |
| Theme preference (system, light or dark) | On your device only | Remember your choice |

You can delete this data at any time by deleting the exported files, or by uninstalling the app, which removes its local storage.

## Camera and photos (Android)

The Field Measurement tool can use the camera, or a photo you pick, to estimate a length from a reference object such as an A4 sheet or bank card. The image is processed on your device to compute the measurement and is not uploaded, stored by us, or shared. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo, and you can deny or revoke it in system settings. The rest of the app works without it.

## Network access

FieldCalc does not connect to the internet. No data leaves your device through the app.

## Children

FieldCalc is a professional engineering tool. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
